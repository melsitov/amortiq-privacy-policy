1Introduction

Amortiq ("the App", "we", "us") is a loan payoff tracker. It keeps a record of the payments you actually make on a mortgage or loan and projects the rest of the schedule from your real balance. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how it is stored, and who can reach it.

The short version is unusually short: the App is built to work entirely offline, and it does not have the technical means to send your information anywhere.

2Data stored on your device

Everything you enter is stored locally on your device, inside the App's private data directory, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is uploaded, automatically or otherwise.

  • Loans — the name you give each loan, currency, amount and balance, interest rate, term, payment day, and any rows you copy from your bank's schedule.
  • Payments — scheduled payments you mark as paid, payments of a different amount, extra payments, and balance updates taken from your bank.
  • Settings — your preferences, and the backup folder and schedule if you set one up.

The App does not ask for your bank login, account numbers, or any identity documents, and it never connects to your bank.

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see section 5).

3No network access

Amortiq does not request the Android INTERNET permission. Android therefore prevents the App from opening any network connection at all. This is not a promise about how we behave — it is a restriction enforced by your operating system, and you can verify it yourself in the App's permission list.

There are no servers involved in running Amortiq, because there is nothing for the App to talk to. We never receive your data, so we cannot lose it, share it, or be compelled to hand it over.

4No third-party services

The App contains no advertising network, no analytics library, no crash reporting, and no marketing or attribution software of any kind. No third-party code in the App collects information about you.

What Amortiq does not do
Show advertisements or use an advertising identifier
Track your usage, sessions, or behaviour
Collect crash reports or diagnostic telemetry
Sell, rent, or share information with anyone
Create an account or profile for you

5Backup and restore files

Amortiq can save all your loans and payments to a backup file, and restore them from one — for example on a new phone. It can also write backups automatically, on a schedule, into a folder you pick, keeping only the most recent few.

These are actions you take deliberately. When you save a backup, your device's own file picker asks you where to put it, and that choice is entirely yours. Once written, the file is an ordinary document on your device, governed by whatever you subsequently do with it.

We have no access to these files and do not receive a copy. If you keep backups in a folder that a cloud service syncs, or send one to someone, that service's privacy policy applies to it, not ours.

Android device backup. If you have turned on backup for your device in Android settings, Android may include the App's data in that backup, stored in your own Google account and handled by Google under its privacy policy. The App itself sends nothing; you can turn this off in your device's backup settings.

6Device permissions

Amortiq asks for no Android runtime permissions — no camera, no location, no contacts, no storage access.

Task How it works without a permission
Saving a backup Uses the Android system save dialog, which writes only where you choose
Restoring from a backup Uses the Android system file picker, which grants access to the single file you pick
Automatic backups Uses the Android system folder picker, which grants access to that one folder only; you can revoke it at any time

If a future version adds payment reminders, the App will ask your permission before showing notifications. Reminders are scheduled and shown on your device only.

7Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You are in complete control of it:

  • Delete individual loans or payment entries from inside the App.
  • Clear the App's storage from your device settings to remove everything at once.
  • Uninstall the App, which deletes its database entirely.

Backup files you saved stay where you put them until you delete them. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf and no deletion request for you to make.

8Children's privacy

Amortiq is intended for adults who are repaying a loan. It is not directed at children, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age.
